Tape Rallies On A Moth At The Podium, And Tommy Called The Buy Signal From The Coffee Station At 9:47 AM

Tommy, a floor trader who has been standing near the same spot on the trading floor for 22 years, saw the moth enter the pit at 9:47, clear the podium by approximately two inches, and exit stage left. Tommy called it a buy signal. The desk agreed. The tape rallied. Tommy is now the floor&rsquo;s top performer by year-to-date return, and the moth has not been seen since.

Tommy was standing near the coffee station on the trading floor at 9:47 AM when he saw the moth enter the pit from the west side, cross the airspace above the podium at an elevation of approximately six feet, and depart through the east exit without touching any surfaces. Tommy has been on this floor for 22 years. He has seen a lot of things move a tape. He has never seen a moth move one, but he knew, the way a guy who has been in a room for 22 years knows, that this was the moment.

Tommy turned to the desk and said “moth.” The desk asked what he meant. Tommy said there was a moth, it was at the podium, and it left. The desk asked whether the moth had landed. Tommy said it had not. The desk asked whether it had been spooked. Tommy said it had not been spooked, it had concluded its business and exited. The desk asked what Tommy wanted to do. Tommy said buy. The desk bought.

“The moth cleared the podium by two inches and exited stage left at 9:47 AM. That is a textbook breakout pattern — I have been on this floor 22 years and a moth does not lie.”

— Tommy, who has been on the floor 22 years

The tape rallied through the morning on the moth’s trajectory, which analysts later described as a clean breakout above the opening range. Tommy was interviewed by a financial-news desk at 11:30 and asked to describe his methodology. Tommy said he saw a moth, it looked confident, and he bought. The interviewer asked whether Tommy had considered the moth’s destination. Tommy said the moth’s destination was not the trade. The moth’s presence was the trade, and the departure was the confirmation.

The moth has not been seen since 9:47 AM. The desk has posted a photo of the empty podium above Tommy’s terminal with a caption that says “the setup.” Tommy has not made another trade since the moth, on principle. He confirmed he is waiting for the next signal, which could be anything, and that he will know it when he sees it, because he has been in this room for 22 years, and the room, at this point, tells him what it wants.
