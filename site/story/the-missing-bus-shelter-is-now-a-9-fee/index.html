The Missing Bus Shelter Is Now a $9 Fee

The stop is a bench and the fare is up for the cover.

TNTessa Nguyen
Economy & Data Reporter 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Kelechi Obi, 38, rides the 44 twice a day, and the fare is up to $3 this spring while the shelter at the corner stop was removed in December, so he waits at a bare bench in the rain for the more expensive bus. The transit board calls the $0.50 fare bump a service investment and does not list the shelter among the investments. He has stood at the bare bench through two downpours.

The board calls it ridership pricing. Kelechi calls it paying for a roof that used to be there. The fare and the shelter moved in opposite directions, and the direction of the money is the only part of the route that improved. The bench is bolted where the shelter stood. Shit, the 44 still comes.

Pays: Kelechi. The shelter is gone.
