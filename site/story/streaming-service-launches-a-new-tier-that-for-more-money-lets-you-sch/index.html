Streaming Service Launches A New Tier That, For More Money, Lets You Scheme To Leave, Which Is The Only Tier Anyone Uses

The service, having noticed that its most popular plan was the one people used to plot their departure, has formalized it as a premium tier: ExitMax, for a modest increase, lets subscribers watch the platform, watch competitors, and watch a countdown of their own cancellation, which the platform confirmed is the feature with, by far, the highest renewal surprise.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2 min read

If you have ever sat on a streaming platform with your thumb hovering over the cancel button, wondering whether the button was part of the subscription, you are, it turns out, their target customer, and you have been paying for the privilege of hovering, which is this quarter's lesson.

The new tier, ExitMax, priced above the standard plan, bundles everything the platform's analytics team identified as the actual behavior of its longest-tenured subscribers: sitting, looking at the catalog, opening another tab, and occasionally typing a password. The platform notes that ExitMax subscribers engage, on average, the most, because exiting is a goal, and a goal, unlike a show, has a completion.

The pitch, delivered by the platform's chief of retention, frames cancellation not as leaving but as the final, richest episode, one the platform will be there for, in the background, should the subscriber care to replay it. Discomfort, the briefing notes, bills reliably, and reluctantly renewing to avoid a morning phone call is, in the retention model, a full season.

Early signups are strong. The platform attributes the uptake to its decision to make the exit button the most expensive thing on the screen, which, it confirms, is now the most-viewed object in its entire catalog, ahead of every show it has ever financed, a statistic the platform cites as a win for content.

The platform declined to say whether the cancel button is inside the paywall, citing a commitment to the next earnings, which it expects to be its best, on the strength of everyone deciding to leave in the spring.
