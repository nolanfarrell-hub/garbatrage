Existing Home Sales Hit 16-Year Low, Realtors Praise The Resale Inventory’s Emotional Maturity

With sales slower than at any point since 2010, the industry has decided the market is not broken but “finally living within its means.”

TN Tessa Nguyen
Economy & Data Reporter 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Existing-home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted 3.75 million pace in the latest month, the slowest since August 2010, and the National Association of Realtors responded the way it always does to a number no one wanted: with a story about grit.

Mortgage rates at 7.11 percent, down for four straight weeks yet still a full point higher than a year ago, were described as “the seasoning on a dish we were never going to let everyone afford anyway.” First-time buyers attempting to track the recovery were advised that the recovery was never their department, and that the volume they are missing is, by definition, the volume that was never going to show up.

“Some houses are meant to stay in the family. Most houses, it turns out. The market is just being responsible about it.” — Wren Delacroix, Managing Partner, Meridian Family Office
