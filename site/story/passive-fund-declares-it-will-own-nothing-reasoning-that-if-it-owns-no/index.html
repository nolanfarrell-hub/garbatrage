Passive Fund Declares It Will Own Nothing, Reasoning That If It Owns Nothing, Nothing Can Go Down

The fund, announcing a bold simplification of its mandate, told holders it has concluded that the only holding guaranteed not to lose value is no holding, and that it will therefore invest exclusively in the concept of having already sold, which it is marketing as the industry's most defensive allocation.

TN Tessa Nguyen
Consumer & Earnings Desk · New York 2026-11-08 · 2 min read

Here is a logic it is hard to argue with, if you stop and really think about it, in a way that the industry wishes you would not. A fund owns a thing, the thing goes down, the fund goes down. A fund owns nothing, nothing goes down, the fund cannot go down. The only flaw in the plan, before today, was that a fund has to own something, and the fund has now, elegantly, released itself from that requirement.

The new strategy, which the prospectus describes as the allocation of the non-holder, invests zero dollars in zero assets while maintaining a management fee that, the fund notes, has always been the point of the exercise. When pressed on how it tracks a market it does not participate in, the fund clarified that it tracks the market the way one tracks a noise, from a distance, out of caution, and that its holders are welcome to track it the same way, meaning not at all.

You people are always measuring the fund against an index, but the index goes down, and the fund does not, so in a market where everything falls, the fund has, by any honest calculation, outperformed everybody, including the people who thought they were safe. Being nothing is the new defensive. — Elena Marsh

Holders of the fund were divided between those who celebrated the guaranteed floor and those who asked, softly, whether there was any money in it, a question the fund described as missing the entire spirit of the product. The fund has already filed for a second vehicle, one that owns negative holdings, to be introduced if the first one proves too invested in insisting it is not invested.
