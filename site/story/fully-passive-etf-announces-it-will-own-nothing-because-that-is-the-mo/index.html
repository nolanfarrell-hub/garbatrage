Fully Passive ETF Announces It Will Own Nothing, Because That Is The Most Passive Position Anyone Has Taken

The fund, which will hold no securities, no cash and no cargo of any kind, confirmed its benchmark is the score the market would have received had it declined to answer, a figure the fund computes daily and declines to show. Officials called the approach not investing but posture, and confirmed the office.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2 min read

There is a fund now that owns nothing. Not a little something. Nothing. It has no stocks, no bonds, no coins in a desk drawer, not even a paperclip, although the fund admits a paperclip would have been a nice touch.

The fund's manager, asked what it tracks, explained that it tracks the market's would-be result, the one the market would have posted had it spent the afternoon not participating, which the manager says is a far more honest number, and also, conveniently, does not require any participation to compute.

Investors have signed up in record numbers, because a fund that owns nothing cannot lose you money, and also cannot make you any, which the manager confirmed was a feature and asked the questioner to read the prospectus, which disclosed, in full, a single word.

When asked how the fund generates a fee on a position of nothing, the manager explained that the fee is also nothing, and that the two had been canceling each other out for years, financially, a development the manager called the first true equilibrium the market had ever seen, and a personal milestone.

The fund closed up on the day, technically, because everything closed up on the day, and the manager, having reviewed the tape, confirmed the fund had outperformed, by not being there.
