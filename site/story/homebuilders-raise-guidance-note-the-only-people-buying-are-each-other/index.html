Homebuilders Post Strong Quarter, All Sales Were Between Builders, Nobody Is Sure Where the Houses Go From Here

Mike Delaney sold a house to Dave Chen on Tuesday. Dave Chen sold a house to Mike Delaney on Wednesday. Both men reported record revenue. Both men are very tired.

TNTessa Nguyen
Consumer & Earnings Desk · New York 2026-11-05 · 2 min read

Mike Delaney builds houses. Dave Chen also builds houses. On Tuesday, Mike sold Dave a house. On Wednesday, Dave sold Mike a house. Both transactions closed in cash. Both builders reported the sales as revenue. Neither builder has moved into the house he bought, because the house he bought was built by the guy who bought his house, and neither of them is entirely sure where the actual families are supposed to go.

"The market is robust," said Mike, standing in a model home that neither he nor Dave has ever slept in. "I bought a thing Dave made. Dave bought a thing I made. That is two transactions. That is economic activity. If you count them in the right order, the sector is growing."

“I sold Dave a house on Tuesday and Dave sold me a house on Wednesday. That is two transactions and the sector is growing — nobody needs to live in them.”

— Mike, who bought Dave's house

Mike and Dave had lunch at a diner between closings. They sat in a booth and talked about lumber costs and interest rates and whether the guy who buys the house next would also be one of them. "I think it's going to be Bob from Pulte," Dave said. "Bob owes me a favor from '22. I sold him a porch he never used. He can sell it back to me next quarter."

Mike nodded. The waitress came by. Mike ordered the meatloaf. Dave ordered the same thing. They split the check, which both of them will report as a joint venture expense, which is a thing you can do when the only person you do business with is yourself, wearing a different hat.

Guidance raised. Demand called "robust." The houses exist. Someone owns them. That someone is either Mike or Dave, and they are working on making it both of them, alternately, forever.
