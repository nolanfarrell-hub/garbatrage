The Hauler Bills the Curb and Keeps the Truck

The monthly rate is up $38. The can does not get lighter.

MRMarcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-18 · 4 min read

Rosa Nduta, 62, lifts her can to the curb on Thursday the way she always has, and the hauler bills her $38 more this month than last. The truck still stops, the arm still lifts, and the can goes into the same hopper. The company says the rate now covers the route. Rosa asks which end of the route the money covers.

The tidy haul is the one thing she pays for outright, after the tax and the county fee and the bag surcharge. She keeps the receipt in the drawer with the ones from last year, and the numbers do not match. Last year the same can cost her $19 off the curb. This year the number is up again, and the service is not. Her neighbor pays the same rate and leaves the same three bags, and Rosa gives him her receipt to hold up next to his own.

“If the truck comes either way, the billing is what you notice, $38 at a time. The route covers itself.”

— Rosa Nduta, 62, who lives off the route

Rosa lifts the can with both hands and pays it anyway. The route covers someone, and it is not her route. Fuck.

Pays: Rosa. The can is heavier.
