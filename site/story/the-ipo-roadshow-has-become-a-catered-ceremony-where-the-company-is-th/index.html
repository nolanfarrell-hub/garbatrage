The IPO Roadshow Has Become A Catered Ceremony Where The Company Is There In Spirit And The Investors Are There For The Sandwich

The banker confirmed the roadshow's real deliverable was never the story, which was on a slide, but the room, which featured a sandwich. The company's CEO, present for the opening toast, departed for a call and did not return, a schedule that multiple investors, plate in hand, described as the strongest signal of the entire offering.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-03 · 2 min read

I have attended my share of roadshows, and I am here to tell you the entire thing now runs on a single unspoken agreement: the investors show up for the sandwich, the company shows up for the logo, and the sandwich, generously, shows up for everyone.

The CEO was present for eleven minutes, the length of the opening toast, the length of the first slide, and roughly the length of one handshake per table, before departing for a call. The bankers, unbothered, explained that the story had been fully loaded, which is banker for the logo is on the screen and the numbers are in the deck, and that the CEO's presence, while nice, was a garnish, which everyone agreed it was, having now said sandwich twice and garnished a third.

Investors, interviewed with plates balanced on the paperwork they did not read, praised the offering's clarity, its vision, and its egg salad, in that order, and confirmed they had participated in the round, by which some meant the offering and most meant the sandwich line, which returned a healthier yield.

The company priced near the top of its range, a result the bankers attributed to the narrative and which the kitchen, more modestly, attributed to the warm roll. One analyst asked, during the quiet part, whether anyone had asked a question about the business, and the room, after a pause, declined to answer, citing the presence of the dessert table.

The shares began trading up, and the sandwich, already gone, was declared, in the offering memorandum, the item most frequently subscribed.
