Private Equity Buys The Hardware Store, Then Explains Why The Owner Was On The Receiving End Of The Supportive Part

An investor group has acquired a hundred-year-old family hardware store, its inventory, and the institutional patience to wait out the founder's decline in the role of a helpful advisor.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-11 · 2 min read

A buyout group announced it has taken control of a beloved local hardware store, promising to keep the name, the staff, and, critically, to keep the founder's name off the deed.

The group spoke warmly of the founder's legacy, which they will be selling back to the community at a markup, and of the store's values, which after the transaction will comprise the square footage and the loading dock.

We are not buying a store. We are buying the feelings people have about a store, and those, unlike the store, are not depreciating. – Marcus Reid

The founder, retained in the role of goodwill, was photographed smiling, which the group confirmed was contractual.
