Family Offices Become the Largest Source of Commercial Real Estate Credit, Confirm Which Families Were Going to Own It Anyway

Wealthy families now fund forced sales at a discount, a role regulators described as inevitable and sellers described as generous, eventually.

PS Priya Sharma
Private Capital Reporter 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Family offices have become the largest provider of commercial real estate financing in the country, stepping into a gap left by traditional lenders by picking up construction loans and forced sales at a discount, according to private-markets data published this month.

The shift completes a long journey in which the same families first loaned developers the money, then the hours, and finally the properties themselves, all while describing their role as "capital on standby."

We did not enter real estate because it was cheap. We entered because the people who owned it finally stopped pretending it was worth what they thought.

Banks, still nursing the office portfolio they overvalued, described the family offices as unexpectedly liquid, then as generous, and finally, this quarter, as the owners.

Analysts said the arrangement guarantees the properties a patient, private, and reasonably unlimited bidder, provided the families agree, which the properties, at the current discount, have shown every willingness to do.

— Barrett Quinn, Buyout Principal
