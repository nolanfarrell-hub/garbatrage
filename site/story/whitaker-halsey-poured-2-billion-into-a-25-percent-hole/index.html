Whitaker Halsey Poured $2 Billion Into a 25% Hole

A falling number is an invitation, and he answered.

TRUE FIGURE
$2B in, -25% on the year
The conviction that arrives to meet the loss.
TNTessa Nguyen
Economy & Data Reporter 2026-11-19 · 2 min read

Whitaker Halsey poured $2 billion into a bitcoin ETF that lost a quarter of its value this year, because the ultra-wealthy treat a falling number the way a shoebox treats a pair of shoes it has already decided it likes: as an invitation. The fund fell 25 percent and the money arrived to meet it. The buyers call it conviction. What it is is a holding pattern around a number that has not yet come back, maintained at enormous expense by people who cannot stand to miss the turn. Shit, the turn will come when it comes.
