Woman Paid $55 Million in Fees Without Reading One Agreement

Aurelia Somerset paid fees for 24 years, would like the money to know it is safe.

JSJake Sullivan
Breakfast Anchor · Before The Open 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Aurelia Somerset, 63, has paid $2.3 million a year in management fees for 24 years without reading the agreements, and has described the payments as the light she leaves on so the money knows it is safe. The money is in two buildings, a trust, and a desk she visits quarterly. She has confirmed the money has never once required a light to find any of them.

The fees total roughly $55 million over the course of her adult life, a figure the advisor's firm confirmed without embarrassment. Ms. Somerset has never asked what the fee is for, and confirmed she does not intend to start asking, because in her experience asking what a thing is for is the fastest way to find out the thing was for her.

Ms. Somerset said that as long as there is a building the money is in, she feels fine. The building is managed by the firm that charges the fee. She said that was the arrangement and that she had stopped interrogating the arrangement the year the money lost 18 percent and the bollocks fee still arrived, which she said was the money's way of staying in touch.

She keeps the quarterly statements stacked on the desk the money is in, unopened, which she said was the desk's entire purpose. When asked what she thinks the money is doing, Ms. Somerset said the money is where it is supposed to be, and she does not check on money that knows where it belongs, because a person who checks on money is a person who does not trust it, and a person who does not trust it is a person who did not leave the light on, which is bollocks.

Aurelia Somerset pays the fees.
