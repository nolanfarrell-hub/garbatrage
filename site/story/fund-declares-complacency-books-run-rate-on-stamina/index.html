Fund Declares Complacency, Books Run Rate on Stamina

The fund sells the calm as growth.

DWDana Whitfield
Portfolio Strategy 2026-11-17 · 4 min read

Trent Farber watched the VIX print 21.51 and felt the calm as a draft coming off his retirement account. He has $17,000 in a fund that bills its persistence as a skill. At run rate, the fund converts a flat tape into stamina, then books the stamina as the asset: subscribers pay for the staying calm, and the calm has held for eleven straight sessions. The firm calls it the patience product. It sells the ability to not react as the ability to grow. He saw the ledger: the staying calm, capitalized, at the same price a volatility fund would charge for the lurching. 'We don't need the tape to move,' said the portfolio director. 'We need the acolyte to believe it won't move.' The roundest figures in finance are the ones that describe nothing happening. He paid $200 a year to watch the market do nothing, and he told his wife the flatness was the yield. There is no separating the product from the patience. The patience is the product.

“We don't need the tape to move. We need the acolyte to believe it won't.”

— Trent Farber
