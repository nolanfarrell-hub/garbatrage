Passive Fund Announces It Will Only Track Things That Have Already Happened

The strategy committee, after months of deliberation, has decided that the future is where money is made but also, and this is critical, where it is lost, and has therefore instructed the index to simply follow the past, which is both safe and already over.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets & Funds · NYSE Floor 2026-11-05 · 2 min read

So there's this fund now, and what it does is, it waits. That's really all it does, is it waits for the thing to happen and then it goes and stands where the thing happened. Like a guy at a party arriving right as everyone's putting on their coats to get a ride somewhere else.

The product, a new ETF that tracks only what has already occurred, has gathered $1.2 billion in its first week, which tells you everything you need to know about the appetite for certainty in a world that will not be giving you any.

"If you can't know the future, the next best thing is to love the past," said Elena Marsh. "And this fund loves it. It loves it all the way back."

Management is candid that the strategy cannot make anyone rich, but points out — and honestly, this is where they got me — that it has never, not once, lost money by being too early.

It does not think you're those other people. It just doesn't think about you at all.
