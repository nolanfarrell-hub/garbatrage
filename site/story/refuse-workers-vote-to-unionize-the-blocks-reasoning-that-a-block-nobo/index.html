Refuse Workers Vote To Unionize The Blocks, Reasoning That A Block Nobody Can Move Is A Member

The trucking outfit, facing a labor action, has convened its own union, composed of the street locations it serves, who have voted, through a representative in attendance, to refuse pickup until the company recognizes the dignity of the curb.

TN Tessa Nguyen
Operations Desk 2026-11-07 · 2 min read

In an attempt to preempt a strike, the company flew organizers to its most troublesome neighborhoods and asked the residents, in the warmest corporate register it had, what they would like the trucks to respect. The residents, to the company's surprise, answered.

The resulting union, Blocks of the Third Ward, has no dues and no officer class, only a shared position, which is that the garbage will be collected when the people who collect it feel that the collection means something, a standard the company has struggled to meet because its own strategy session kept deferring to the trash.

You cannot scab a curb. You can hire all the drivers you want, but the curb knows, and the curb is not choosing a side, the curb is simply declining to schedule pickup until everyone who asks it for pickup respects what it is carrying.” — Elena Marsh

The standoff continues, with the blocks holding firm, the company sending increasingly generous offers, and the refuse, remarkably, staying exactly where it was, which both sides now recognize as the most honest negotiation the district has ever seen.
