Fund Sits on Dry Powder, Discovers the Anxiety of Having It

Eleven months dry, and the money still asks why.

MRMarcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-17 · 4 min read

Marcus Beale, 54, sat on $340 million of dry powder for the eleventh month and felt the performance anxiety arrive like a thing he had to introduce at a party. He is a partner at a fund that raised the money to deploy it, and the money is ready, loaded, waiting — and every time he swings at a deal, the deal looks back at him the way a man looks at another man's enthusiasm in a dim room. The partners called it dry powder. Around month nine, money that has not been used starts to question the man holding it. He can deploy at any moment. That is the problem. The ability to act has become the reason he does not. His own fund has begun to measure him by the untouchedness of the pile, as though the money being ready is itself the strategy, and the longer it stays ready the more ready it is. Marcus has started checking the balance at night, the way you check on an investment you are afraid has become a hostage. "We are sitting on a powder keg of opportunity," he told the limited partners, and the keg is the thing he is most afraid to light. He has eleven months of dryness and no idea what the money is for, only that the money is still there, and that the staying there has become the whole performance.

“We are sitting on $340 million of powder keg opportunity.”

— Marcus Beale
