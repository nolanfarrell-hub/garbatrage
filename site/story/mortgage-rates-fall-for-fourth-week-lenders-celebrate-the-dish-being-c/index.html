Mortgage Rates Fall For Fourth Week, Lenders Celebrate The Dish Being Cheaper For The People Who Never Owned The Recipe

A 7.11 percent is quietly described as relief, with the caveat that relief, like everything else, was always going to be relative.

TN Tessa Nguyen
Economy & Data Reporter 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Thirty-year mortgage rates eased for a fourth straight week to 7.11 percent, a decline described as welcome, though the word “relief” was deployed only after a note specifying that relief is a function of how much pain you were previously told to sit still through.

A year ago the same loan fetched 6.24 percent, a number industry analysts invoked roughly the way a draftsman invokes a blueprint for a building that was never going to house the client either.

“They told me the rate went down, and then showed me the new payment, and the new payment had gone up because the price had, and I think that’s what they mean by a market that learns.” — Delia Osei, Evening Custodian
