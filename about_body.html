About

Garbatrage is a satirical financial publication. That is the whole answer, and we are aware we have now written it in several places. The rest of this page exists because people keep asking a second question, and the second question is usually "but which parts."

What this is

A daily financial news desk, in the visual and typographic register of the real thing, reporting on a market that does not exist. We write about the Garbatrage Industrial Average and the twenty-five companies in it. We write about rate policy, fund structures, family offices and hauling volumes. We do it deadpan, on time, and without winking at the reader mid-sentence.

The register is the joke. Finance already talks like this. We mostly just refuse to stop.

What is invented

Everything, unless marked otherwise.

  • Headlines, articles, quotes, sources, companies, indexes, tickers and figures are fabricated.
  • The anchors are fictional people with fictional beats.
  • Where real market prices appear — the tape at the top of the page — those are real, supplied by third parties, and can be delayed or wrong.
  • Any resemblance to real firms or people is either coincidence or deliberate, and is not a factual claim either way.
Nothing here is investment advice. We are not a registered adviser, a broker, or an exchange. Do not act on it. See the Terms.

Who makes it

Garbatrage is written and edited by a small desk, and is machine-assisted. A human writes and edits every published piece; machine tooling is used for drafting, art and production. We say so plainly because a lot of sites don't.

The bylines are fictional. The desk behind them is not.

Corrections

We correct errors in the fiction — a ticker that contradicts an earlier one, a company with two founding dates, an anchor who dies and then files. Corrections are appended to the piece, not silently made. Send them to info@garbatrage.com with the URL.

We do not correct the satire. If the joke is wrong, that's a different department.

Ownership and funding

Independently owned by Garbatrage Media Group LLC. No outside investment, no parent company, no sponsor yet.

When there is sponsored content, it will carry a label at the top of the piece and in the tape. When there are affiliate links, they will be disclosed above the fold. Neither exists today.
