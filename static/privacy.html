Privacy Policy

We barely know anything about you, and what we know we don't want. That's the policy, in full, forever. — The version below is longer because several of our suppliers insist on it.

This policy explains what Garbatrage Media Group LLC ("we") collects when you visit garbatrage.com, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Short version

There are no accounts, no logins, and no first-party tracking cookies. We don't build profiles and we don't sell data. What exists is ordinary server logging plus a handful of third-party embeds that come with their own identifiers.

3. Why

  • To serve the site — routing, caching, security, abuse prevention (legitimate interest).
  • To count readers — aggregate, cookieless (legitimate interest).
  • To send the newsletter — if and only if you asked (consent, and performance of our agreement with you).
  • To fix things — diagnosing errors and load (legitimate interest).

4. Cookies

We set no first-party cookies. Third-party embeds may set their own; TradingView in particular may. Where a non-essential third-party cookie is set and you are in the UK or EEA, consent is requested before it is used. You can block cookies in your browser without breaking the site, though some widgets may not load.

5. Retention

Server logs: 30 days. Aggregate analytics: 6 months. Newsletter address: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days to honour the request.

6. Sharing

We share data with the processors listed in §2, and with our email provider if you subscribe. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. If that changes — for example if we take advertising — this section will change first, and before the change takes effect.

7. Your rights (UK/EEA)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Write to info@garbatrage.com. We respond within one month. You may also complain to your national supervisory authority.

8. Your rights (California)

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of sale or sharing, and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights. We do not currently sell or share personal information as those terms are defined. Requests: info@garbatrage.com, subject line "CCPA."

9. International transfers

Our processors are mostly US-based, so data reaches the United States. Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision where required.

10. Security

The site is served over TLS via Cloudflare. No system is perfect, and we hold very little worth stealing.

11. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Do Not Track

Because we set no first-party tracking cookies, DNT is largely satisfied by default. We do not currently alter behaviour in response to the header.

13. Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date, and flagged on the Site for 30 days.
