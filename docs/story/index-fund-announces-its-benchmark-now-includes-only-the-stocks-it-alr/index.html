Index Fund Outperforms Benchmark By Exactly Zero Percent Forever After Rewriting Benchmark To Be Itself

The fund got tired of losing to a benchmark it could not control. So it changed the benchmark to match its portfolio. It now outperforms itself by exactly zero percent forever. The managers call this perfection. Competitors call it cheating. Regulators call it 'creative.'

MRMarcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-07 · 2 min read

Imagine you are in a race, and you are losing. So you move the finish line to where you are standing. That is what this fund did. And they announced it on a conference call. With applause.

The fund's managers were looking at a wall of charts and realized the thing they were supposed to beat no longer existed. The market had changed. Stocks came and went. The benchmark was a ghost. So they did the logical thing: they edited the benchmark until it looked exactly like their own portfolio. Problem solved.

Now the fund outperforms its benchmark by exactly zero percent, forever. It can never lose to the benchmark, because the benchmark is the fund. The fund's website now says 'we have never underperformed our index.' This is technically true. The index, you see, follows the fund around like a puppy.

“The fund has never underperformed the benchmark. That is a fact in black and white on the prospectus page. If the benchmark follows the fund now, that just means the fund is leading. You do not complain about the thing that is following you.”

— an investor who checked the prospectus and approved

Rick Castellano, a retiree in Toledo who has his nest egg in this fund, was asked how he feels about the change. Rick said he does not understand what happened, but his quarterly statement looks better than last year. The fund says Rick is the reason they did it. Rick does not know he is the reason. Rick is just happy the graph goes up.
