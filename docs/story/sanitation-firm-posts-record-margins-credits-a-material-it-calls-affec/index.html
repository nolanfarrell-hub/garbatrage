Sanitation Firm Posts Record Margins, Credits A Material It Calls, Affectionately, The Load

In a quarter of logistics excellence, the hauler reported its most profitable run ever, a margin it attributes to a consistency of collection that has, for some of its customers, become their entire curbside biography.

EM Elena Marsh
Head of Research 2026-11-11 · 2 min read

A regional hauler celebrated record profitability, and the trade press, reaching for the how, was handed a single word: the load, which the company explained is the industry term for the thing it gets paid to take, and which, on certain routes, it has now carried so many times it considers recurring.

Analysts attribute the margin to route efficiency, route density, and a customer base that generates, in a phrase the company used without blinking, a remarkably consistent product.

We are not in the waste business. We are in the relationships business, and some of these relationships have really committed. – Gordon Heath, Chief Executive, Hauler

Inspectors have been invited to verify the load, and have so far declined, which, the company notes, is also a kind of efficiency.
