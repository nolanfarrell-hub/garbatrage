The Dead Plant Took Her Whole Utility Credit

The charge canceled the the winter-heat discount.

CRClaire Reyes
Debt & Fixed Income, plus consumer credit 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Yolanda Adeyemi, 71, qualifies for her state's winter-heat discount, and the utility canceled it this season because an inspector found her window box plant was dry and called the unit not maintained. The discount was $58 a month. The dead plant cost her the winter. She has kept that plant bare for two years and the heat has never once canceled for it until the discount became the thing they inspect for. The utility calls it a service condition. Yolanda calls it the fine print now reaching into the flower box. The plant was never part of the heat agreement; it became part of the heat agreement the month the money was on the line. She waters the bare stem anyway, because she cannot afford to fail the check twice. "The plant was dead and now the discount is too, and they counted the one against the other." Yolanda waters the stem. Shit, the winter.

Pays: Yolanda. The stem is bare now.
