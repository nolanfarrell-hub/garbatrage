Cost Of Running Your Own Family Office Hits $6.6 Million A Year, And The Wealth Calls It Affordable

The ultimate luxury is reportedly not the fleet or the compound but the $6.6 million annual operating bill of the office that keeps track of both.

EM Elena Marsh
Head of Research 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

JPMorgan’s survey of the world’s largest family offices found that running a single office for a billion-dollar-plus family now costs about $6.6 million a year, up from $6.1 million, and that families considering the expense have reportedly asked one reasonable question: is that enough.

Industry advisers described the figure not as overhead but as membership dues to the only club whose grief is outsourced. About 55 percent of offices surveyed target a 7 to 10 percent return, a range they described as humble and, relative to the household, generous.

“We don’t call it cost anymore. We call it the payroll of the people who make sure we never hear the word cost in a room with windows.” — Josephine Mercouris, Third-Generation Heir
