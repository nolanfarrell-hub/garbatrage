Hedge Fund Announces It Is Shorting Tomorrow; Position Open, Delivery To Be Determined

A fund with one of the best letterheads in the industry has opened a sizable short against the entire concept of tomorrow, arguing that tomorrow has rallied for decades on nothing but expectation, and that it is, therefore, long overdue for a correction.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-09 · 2 min read

A Manhattan firm that manages, by its own count, more than it should, has confirmed that its flagship strategy is now a short position on tomorrow, which it holds at maximum size and expects to cover on, as the prospectus puts it, a date that does not yet exist.

We reached the firm's head of forward logistics, who explained the thesis while checking whether the conference room was, in fact, the room. The future, he said, has been priced to perfection for as long as anyone can remember, which he conceded is not long, because no one can remember the future. When asked what happens when tomorrow arrives and the short is still open, he said that was the entire point, and that the fund would simply let tomorrow become today, at which point the fund has won, by definition.

"If today keeps happening, we are right in the sense that matters, which is the sense in which we are not out of money yet." — Elena Marsh, Head of Research

Analysts have struggled to model the position, running the scenario where tomorrow never comes, with a notional value so improbable the model returned a friendly warning. The fund, undeterred, has extended the short to the day after tomorrow, which it describes as tomorrow-squared and therefore twice as overpriced.
