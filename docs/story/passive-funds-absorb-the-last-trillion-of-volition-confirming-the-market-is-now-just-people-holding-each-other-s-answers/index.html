Passive Funds Absorb The Last Trillion Of Volition, Confirming The Market Is Now Just People Holding Each Other's Answers

With index funds owning the tape, executives celebrated the final transfer of decision-making from humans to a spreadsheet that is not watching them, and is not required to.

DW Dana Whitfield
Portfolio Strategy 2026-11-11 · 2 min read

The inflows arrived again, and this time there was no one left to take the other side, because the other side was also an index, holding a slightly different version of the same nothing.

It is the quietest consensus in history: everyone has decided to own everything, which requires deciding nothing, which is the point, and the point now owns the market.

Executives compare the feat to a library that owns every book and therefore no longer needs readers, only a card saying the library is full, and a fee for shelving.

The one remaining active manager, asked for his outlook, said he had an outlook, and then the outlook was priced in, and then the outlook was the index, and then the outlook had a fee.

The market now consists of people holding each other, which is not a market but a mood, and the mood is buying.

“Nobody has to be right about anything anymore. You just buy the average of everyone being wrong, and the average is always up, which is the quietest Ponzi there is.” – Lenora Vane, Index Fund Trustee
