Fed Chair Assumes Commuters Helicopter to Work, Blames Bus Fare Hike on Increased Rotor Maintenance

Intracity transit fares rose 6.6% in a single month — the largest monthly increase since 2020, annualized to over 100%. The Federal Reserve attributes the spike to rising rotor-maintenance costs.
