New Fund Will Only Invest in Things That Have Already Happened, Raises $1.2 Billion From People Who Are Done Being Surprised

Phil Morrison's new ETF does not predict the future. It does not try to predict the future. It does not acknowledge the future. The future is where money gets lost. The past is where money sits, quietly, having already been accounted for.

MRMarcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-05 · 2 min read

Phil Morrison is a fund manager. Phil Morrison got tired of being wrong. Most fund managers get tired of being wrong and then keep being wrong because that is the job. Phil Morrison had a different idea. Phil Morrison started a fund that only buys things after they have already happened.

"The problem with investing is the future," Phil said. "The future is unpredictable. The past is extremely predictable. I know exactly what happened yesterday. I was there. I have the receipts. So I decided: no more future. We only trade the past."

“I put $340,000 into a fund that only buys stocks that already went up yesterday. You cannot lose money on something that already happened — that is just math.”

— Phil, who is done being surprised

The fund raised $1.2 billion in its first week. Phil was surprised. "I thought people would think it was a joke," he said. "But people are very tired of being wrong. They would rather be late than wrong. Being late is just being right, slower. Being wrong is being wrong. Nobody wants to be wrong."

Phil's wife asked him what happens if the market goes up for a week and then down. Phil said the fund would buy the up and skip the down. She asked if that meant the fund would buy high and miss the lows. Phil said that was not a question the fund's mandate required him to answer. The fund's mandate is to look backward. Looking backward, by definition, does not include the bit where you answer questions about what comes next.

"I think this is the future of investing," Phil said, standing in his office, which has one window and no view of anything that hasn't already happened.
