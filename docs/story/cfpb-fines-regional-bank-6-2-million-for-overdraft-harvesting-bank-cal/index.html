CFPB Fines Regional Bank $6.2 Million For Overdraft Harvesting, Bank Calls It A Cost Of Doing Right By The Branch

Regulators caught a bank charging fees its customers never agreed to; the bank framed the settlement as “mining done responsibly.”

JS Jake Sullivan
Breakfast Anchor · Before The Open 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau ordered Atlantic Union Bank to pay $6.2 million for what it called “illegal overdraft fee harvesting,” the bureaucratic term for charging customers fees they never agreed to and then, per the complaint, appearing normal about it.

The bank acknowledged the fee revenue and disputed the word illegal, preferring “optimized.” Industry analysts noted the harvest reflects an economy that has learned to lean on the household’s hardest month as its own softest quarter.

“The statement said ‘insufficient funds,’ and I had the funds, I just needed them for last week. The bank had a different definition of when I was supposed to be me again.” — Simone Vega, Aide at a Care Facility
