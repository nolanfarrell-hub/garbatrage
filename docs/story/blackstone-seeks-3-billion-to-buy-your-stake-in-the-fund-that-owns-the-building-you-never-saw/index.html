Blackstone Seeks $3 Billion To Buy Your Stake in the Fund That Owns the Building You Never Saw

Secondaries are booming because everyone is trying to leave at once, and someone has to be the door.

JS Jake Sullivan
Private Equity Correspondent 2026-11-13 · 2 min read

Blackstone is raising at least $3 billion for a new fund whose entire purpose is to buy other funds' stakes at a discount, a strategy described by industry veterans as the rare trade that profits from being the way out.

We are not buying buildings, the pitch explains. We are buying the right to stop pretending we own one. It is a cleaner product with a higher return and it never leaks.

The secondaries market has nearly doubled in two years as investors trapped in older funds realize the door they are looking for is a fee. Blackstone intends to be the biggest door, which the market confirms is the only kind worth being.

The fund's success appears all but certain, since it will be filled by the exact investors it exists to rescue, who will describe the transaction afterward as having finally gotten clarity.

You think you own real estate. You own a line on a page. We are not buying the line; we are buying the page, and we got a great price on the book. — Wren Castellan, Secondaries Lead, Meridian Capital
