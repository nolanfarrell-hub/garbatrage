Family Office Compares Notes On ‘Family Discount’, Decides Heredity Is Underpriced

A new report found family-member executives reliably out-earn outside hires, a premium the industry attributes to blood, and the family attributes to clarity.

PS Priya Sharma
Washington Bureau · Fed & Rates 2026-11-14 · 2 min read

Wealth management’s annual census of who gets paid for what found that family-member chief investment officers earn more than outside hires doing the same work, a gap the industry has named the “family discount” and the families have named, with admirable candor, “the point.”

Outside executives are expected to deliver returns; family members are expected to remain, which the report treated as the more valuable credential and the market, at these rates, appears to agree.
