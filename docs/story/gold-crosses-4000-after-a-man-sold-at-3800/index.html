Gold Crosses $4,000 as Preston Sold at $3,800

The gap between $3,800 and $4,000 costs him a summer home.

MRMarcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Preston Cavell, 66, sold twenty kilos of bullion in the spring when it was trading around $3,800 an ounce, on advice from a planner who said the metal had had its run and that his money belonged somewhere newer. The newer place was an AI fund, since down 18 percent, while gold has climbed past $4,000 — a gap between what Preston sold and where it now sits that he has recalculated every day since at roughly the level of a solidly built summer home. He does not need the money. That is not the point, and he will tell you loudly and often while checking the price. The hurt is not the loss. The hurt is that he chose, and the metal that everyone said was finished has spent the whole year pointing at him and being right. The guy who sold safe money for fancier money calls the whole spread shit and keeps staring at it like the metal owes him an apology.

Pays: Preston, once more, having chosen wrong.
