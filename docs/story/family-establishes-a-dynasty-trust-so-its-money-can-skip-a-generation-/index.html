Family Establishes A Dynasty Trust So Its Money Can Skip A Generation It Has Already Written Off

The architecture is simple: wealth intends to outlive its owners, and its founders have decided that the generation currently standing between them and perpetuity is a tax, not a beneficiary.

OZ Omar Zayed
Corporate Governance Desk 2026-11-11 · 2 min read

A prominent family announced it has placed its fortune in a dynasty trust, a structure that allows wealth to skip a generation, and to re-skip it, and to keep skipping until it encounters a descendant judged worthy of the warm bath of compounding.

Sources say the trust is intentionally spendthrift-averse, because the trustees have observed what a generation does with money when it is handed to them warm, and they have decided the money's feelings matter more.

We are not cutting anyone out. We are deferring everyone, in trust, until the family has had time to think. – Omar Zayed

The heirs, informed that the fortune would be held in perpetuity and that they were, in a legal sense, a temporary inconvenience, said they understood, and that their advisors had been expecting something like this, and had already drafted around it.
