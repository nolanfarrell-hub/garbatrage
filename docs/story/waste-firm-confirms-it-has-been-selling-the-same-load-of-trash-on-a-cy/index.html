Waste Firm Confirms It Has Been Selling The Same Load Of Trash On A Cycle, And That The Cycle, Technically, Is Production

The hauler, responding to shareholder questions about volume, clarified that its flagship 'load' has now been counted, sold, recounted, and re-sold across four consecutive quarters, and that this, the company argued, is not fraud, but a circular economy, which it defined as the high art of selling nothing in a loop.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-06 · 2 min read

Now, I want to tell you about a company, and I want you to sit down, because this is the kind of thing we all kind of know is happening, we just never hear anyone say it out loud. A waste management firm has confirmed, in writing, that it has been selling the same load of trash, on a cycle, and that it does not see the problem.

The load — call it Load A, so we can all feel like insiders — was collected in Q1, sold in Q1, recounted in Q2, sold again in Q2, and so on, until it achieved what the company now calls 'steady-state inventory,' which is finance for the same garbage, but with better margins. Shareholders, wearily, have stopped asking what is in the load, because the load, like a lot of things in this economy, is whatever keeps the line moving.

Asked whether selling the same trash every quarter is fraudulent, the company was philosophical. It is only trash if you stop moving it, the CFO said. The moment you sell it on schedule, it is revenue. You would not call a house you live in, live in. And they are right, which is the terrifying part.

“There is honest volume, and there is volume. This company has discovered a third thing, which is the same volume, and I would argue it is the only kind you can actually plan around. You cannot forecast a landfill. You can forecast a load that knows its own schedule.”

The company confirmed its recent 'growth' was attributable entirely to Load A's promotion, and that Load A, for its part, requested a raise, which the board, in a show of support, approved, by selling it one more time. That is just the way it is.
