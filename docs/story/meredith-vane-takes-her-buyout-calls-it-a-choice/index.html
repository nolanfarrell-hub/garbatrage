Meredith Vane Takes Her Buyout, Calls It a Choice

The buyout was voluntary. The runway cost her own salary.

JSJake Sullivan
Breakfast Anchor · Before The Open 2026-11-19 · 4 min read

Meredith Vane, 52, took the buyout from Orbyt the moment the memo made it to her inbox, because the memo said the exits were open and she has always wanted to be first to choose her own door, and it has been her own door since the day the company priced it. They extended the offer twice so enough people would shuffle off on schedule, and each extension was a deadline wearing a sweater, and Meredith called that freedom.

The company calls it employee empowerment. Meredith calls it leaving on her own terms, terms that Orbyt wrote and she signed without reading the clause about her shares, which now vest on a schedule she can feel herself forgetting. She is worth more at this exact moment than she has been in a year, and she cannot spend a dollar of it until the waterfall finishes doing its own laps.

'They gave me the entire runway,' she tells her dinner guests, and the runway is what Orbyt cleared so it could stop paying her salary sooner.

Meredith picked the door she was always facing, and the door is the only thing she got to pick. Shit, she calls it self-directed, this door.

Pays: Meredith Vane.
