Streaming Service Launches A New Tier That Charges Kevin Extra For Hovering Over The Cancel Button, Which Is What Kevin Has Been Doing For 14 Months

Kevin from Phoenix has been hovering over the cancel button on his streaming service every Thursday night for 14 months. He has never clicked it. The service, noticing that the hover is Kevin&rsquo;s most-watched content, has formalized it as ExitMax, a $4.99 premium add-on that Kevin was auto-enrolled in this morning.

MR Marcus Reid
Markets Anchor · NYSE Floor 2026-11-03 · 2 min read

Kevin from Phoenix was sitting on his couch at 9 PM on a Thursday, as he has every Thursday for 14 months, with his thumb hovering over the cancel button on his streaming service, wondering whether the decision to not click counted as a decision, or whether it counted as the subscription continuing by default. It turns out it is a third category called a growth opportunity, and the growth opportunity has been charged to his card.

The streaming service, which has been watching Kevin watch the cancel button, has formalized his entire Thursday-night routine as a premium tier called ExitMax. For an additional $4.99 a month, Kevin can now watch the service, watch the cancel button, watch a countdown of his own renewal, and watch a welcome-back video that begins with an executive saying “we knew you would stay.” Kevin did not sign up for ExitMax. Kevin was enrolled this morning, because the exit Kevin was considering was, in the service’s analysis, the most engaging content in Kevin’s catalog.

“I hover over the cancel button every Thursday night for 14 months now. Four ninety-nine is nothing for the peace of mind that comes from knowing I am still subscribed.”

— Kevin, who has been hovering for 14 months

Kevin called the service to ask how to cancel ExitMax. The representative explained that ExitMax can be canceled from the account settings page, which is located behind a new button called ExitMax Settings, which Kevin can access by first canceling his ExitMax subscription. Kevin asked whether the cancel button for ExitMax is inside ExitMax. The representative confirmed it was, and wished him a great evening.

Kevin sat back down at 9 PM on Thursday with a new monthly charge on his card and the same thumb hovering over the same button, which now costs more to hover over, which Kevin realized was the most honest representation of the relationship he had ever received from the service. He confirmed he would not be canceling that either.
