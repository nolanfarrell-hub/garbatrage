AI Firm Borrows $4 Billion for More Servers; Technician Who Keeps Them Cool Makes $68,000 With a Broken Garbage Disposal

The loan is investment grade. The payoff is a forecast. The forecast is a belief.

JSJake Sullivan
Breakfast Anchor · Before The Open 2026-11-13 · 2 min read

Derek Okonkwo is a data center technician at ExaCompute Holdings. His job is to keep the servers cool. The servers are what the company just borrowed $4 billion to buy more of. Derek was not consulted on the borrowing, although he will be consulted on the cooling, by a loud alarm at 3 a.m.

Derek makes $68,000 a year. The servers he maintains cost $3 million per rack. He knows this because a manager told him once, during a power outage, to treat each rack like it was his own house. Derek thought about his own house. He lives in a two-bedroom apartment with a broken garbage disposal. He treats the racks like they are Yale.

The company borrowed the full $4 billion in one day. The banker who signed the deal was quoted saying the credit story was "the strongest he had heard all year." Derek's credit story is a Visa card with a $4,200 limit and two late payments from last January when he had to choose between the minimum and dinner.

When the servers go down, Derek is the one who drives in. He told his boss once that the company should maybe not borrow so much money for machines that break when the air gets too warm. His boss said that's why they pay Derek. Derek said they pay him $68,000. His boss said the market rate for server technicians is fifty-two and to be grateful.

“Four billion dollars is a lot of servers but the computers are going to figure things out that people cannot. I keep them cool. That is my job. The $4 billion is an investment in the future and I am part of it.”

— a data center technician who believes in the vision

The company says the computers will generate enough revenue to service the debt. Derek says the computers currently generate a lot of heat and a weekly report that nobody reads. He says this quietly, because he likes the health insurance.
